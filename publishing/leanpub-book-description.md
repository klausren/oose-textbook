## Book description 详细描述（Markdown，粘贴进 Leanpub 的 Book Description）

```markdown
### What this book is

Most software engineering textbooks are written for students who read English
as a first language. In an English-medium classroom, the bottleneck is usually
**the English, not the engineering**. This book is written from the other
direction: controlled sentence length, every technical term pre-taught before
it is used, one term for one concept across all 20 chapters, and a *Language
Focus* section at the end of each chapter that teaches the academic English
patterns that chapter needs.

### One case, the whole lifecycle

**CareLink**, a community elder-care platform, is introduced in Chapter 1 and
grows with every chapter. The requirements you read are the requirements you
will model; the models you read are the models you will design from; the design
you read is the design you will test. You never have to hold five unrelated
toy examples in your head at once.

### AI, with verification discipline

Every chapter carries an **AI Companion**:

- what AI is actually good at for *this* chapter's task,
- a copy-paste prompt pattern you can use today,
- a **Verify Before You Trust** checklist — the specific checks that catch
  plausible-looking wrong output,
- one real example of how AI gets this chapter wrong.

Labs run two tracks (manual and AI-assisted) against **one rubric**, so using
AI is neither rewarded nor punished — only the result and your critique of it
are graded. Our position: *AI changes the cost of producing artefacts, not the
responsibility for their correctness.*

### Genuinely illustrated

Target of **9 or more figures per chapter and 180+ in the book**: concept maps,
process diagrams, UML models, before/after contrasts, UI sketches, data
figures, and one AI-workflow figure per chapter — all drawn as versionable SVG
in one visual language, so twenty chapters read as one coherent artifact.

### Built for a 64-hour course

The book maps onto a two-semester sequence (Software Engineering I + II):
20 chapters, roughly one chapter per teaching week, ~380 pages. Every chapter
ends with exercises in **four tiers** (recall, apply, analyse/critique,
AI-augmented) plus a running project task, so the book can be taught without
a separate lab manual.

### Written while it is taught

Chapters are written in the order they are taught, piloted on a real class of
international undergraduates, and revised from what actually confused them.
This is why the book is on Early Access: it is finished in public.
